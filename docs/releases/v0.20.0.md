# v0.20.0 — release verification

Released on 2026-10-20. The browser's everyday keys move to single letters:
`r` renames, or retitles the marked set; `d` deletes, or deletes the marked set;
refresh moves from `r` to `s`. `Ctrl+R`, `Ctrl+D`, and `Ctrl+T` keep working as
hidden aliases. `X` marks or clears only the rows on screen. `/` accepts
`path:folder text` to narrow the list and search to one subfolder of the scope.
Below a 64-column band (a phone over SSH), rows drop the single-agent chip, the
count's unit, and under date bands the `MMDD｜` prefix, and the footer spells out
the selected title over two fixed lines; wider bands are unchanged.
